The Stillmaker froze this shore and everyone on it. Luma, the fox spirit of your hearth, sends you out with your spirit animal. Free every creature, and the shore will wake.

How to play
Move with one thumbDrag anywhere on the screen. Your powers fire on their own.
Free creatures, grab gemsEvery creature you free drops a qi gem. Fill the bar to level up and pick a power.
Five elementsEvery power and creature has one, shown by its colour. Water checks Fire, Fire checks Metal, Metal checks Wood, Wood checks Earth, Earth checks Water: 1.5× damage.
Evolve your powersMax a power to five stars and hold the gift of the element that feeds it. Crystal brutes drop Spirit Chests that help.
Free the shoreSurvive about three minutes as the hordes grow, then defeat the Still Warden.
