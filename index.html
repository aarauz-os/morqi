The story 02

The Stillmaker froze the world.

His frost covered far more than five lands. Everyone stopped, right where they stood.

Its people are stuck in feelings.

In each realm, one person is stuck in one feeling, and the feeling has changed them.

  • Fear
  • Anger
  • Restless excitement
  • Worry
  • Grief

You are a Keeper. Balance brings them back.

Luma, the fox spirit of your hearth, sends you and your spirit animal out to bring the world back to life.

The game 03

Run in. Free them. Bring the world back.

An action game where your powers fire on their own and you move through whole crowds of creatures. Every creature you free turns the frozen ground back into life around you, so progress is something you can see.

  1. 1Run inDrag with one thumb. Your powers and your spirit animal fight beside you, and the frost thaws wherever you have been.
  2. 2Free themEvery creature is weak to one element, the one that keeps it in check. Match it and it frees twice as fast.
  3. 3Bring the world backBeat the realm’s boss, then breathe life back into them.

Playable today on the web: 5 levels (four short realms and a finale of 10 minutes or more), 7 Keepers, 12 spirit animals, 15 creatures, 3 kinds of giant, 5 Star Calls, an original score.

The lesson inside the fun 04

Every level is one element’s world, alive.

Kids learn the five elements of traditional Chinese medicine the way they learn type matchups in any monster game: by playing. Water puts out fire, wood drinks up water, metal chops wood. Using a creature’s weakness earns a star.

The heart 05

Every day 06

Every day starts with a breath.

Around every run sits the Sanctuary, the child’s home in MorQi. Nothing here is sold on its own: every plot, charm and page is earned by playing. All of it is built into the web game today.

MorQi saves to the home screen like an app, with a backup code a grown-up can keep.

  1. Today

    The daily breathThirty seconds of slow breathing with your spirit animal, inspired by Qigong. Then three small goals that only add rewards.

  1. As they play

    A garden that grows with every creature freedEach of the five plots grows with its own element, so a garden in Harmony needs all five. The people you free keep healing in the Freed Book.

  1. Every month

    An Element Weekend, and charms to keepOn the second weekend of each month, one element’s creatures give double qi for the garden. Ten Spirit Charms come from the people you free, the days you breathe and Harmony.
